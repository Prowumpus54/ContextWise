# Project: ContextWise

## Problem
People forget why projects changed, what remains unfinished, and what previous conversations concluded. They need human-readable, source-linked histories that can be browsed at several resolutions without moving their entire private archive into an AI platform.

## P0 product promise
A fast, human-first web app where project histories are browsable, searchable, source-linked, and manually exportable. A zoomed-out view emphasizes milestones; zooming in reveals decisions, blockers and individual notes.

## Non-goals for P0
MCP, agent skills, automated AI ingestion, cloud synchronization, paid accounts, public sharing, blockchain wallets, and custom cryptography.

## Product rules
- Show what is known, uncertain, superseded, or source-unverified.
- Make sharing opt-in, previewable, and project-scoped.
- Default to local, explicit data handling and clear backup/recovery semantics.
- Never describe unencrypted files or in-memory prototypes as secure storage.
